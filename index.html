About

Making Indian air travel transparent

Flight Cost Intelligence turns raw fare data into actionable insights. Instead of guessing whether a ₹5,000 ticket is a good deal, we show you exactly how much you're paying per kilometre — so you can compare routes fairly, regardless of distance.

Built for travellers, analysts, and anyone who wants smarter domestic flight decisions — completely free, no account required.
