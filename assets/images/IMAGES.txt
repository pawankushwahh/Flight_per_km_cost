FLIGHT COST INTELLIGENCE — LOCAL IMAGE GUIDE
================================================
Folder: frontend/assets/images/
Use these exact filenames. Formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, or .webp

General tips for ALL images:
  - Prefer landscape (wide) for heroes and cards; square for avatars
  - Dark or muted photos work best (site uses a dark theme + gradient overlays)
  - Avoid busy text, logos, or watermarks in the image
  - High quality, slightly desaturated photos look most premium
